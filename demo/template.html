03 / The onboard pipeline

From photons to motion.

No GPS, simulator depth, or simulator position enters the rover's navigation controller.

01 / OBSERVE

Stereo RGB

Two synchronised 640 × 400 images, calibrated with a 16 cm baseline.

02 / RECONSTRUCT

Metric depth

SGBM disparity and left–right consistency. Invalid depth remains unknown.

03 / LOCALISE

Visual odometry

Optical-flow tracking, forward–backward checks, and robust PnP motion estimation.

04 / PLAN

Observed ground

Height evidence, persistent obstacle mapping, and A* with footprint inflation.

05 / CONTROL

Guarded motion

Ground support and stopping-envelope checks before differential wheel commands.

What this run establishes

Camera-driven A-to-B navigation around two static obstacles in a flat simulated outdoor clearing. The goal is 8 metres from the known starting pose. Raw logs and an independent evaluator accompany the recording.

What remains to be demonstrated

Learned semantic perception, loop-closing SLAM, dynamic obstacles, slopes, changing weather, and hardware transfer. This controlled demonstration is not evidence of general outdoor robustness.
